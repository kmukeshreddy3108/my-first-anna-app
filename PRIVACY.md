# Privacy Policy for StudyLens AI

**Last updated**: November 2026

StudyLens AI is designed with strict data privacy and local-first principles.

---

### 1. Information Processing & Scope
- **Document Text**: All document ingestion, text parsing (from TXT, MD, CSV, JSON, XML, HTML, Office docs, and text-layer PDFs), and file processing happen locally within your Anna client environment.
- **AI Processing**: When you run an analysis, text chunks are sent to Anna's host LLM sampling bridge strictly to generate grounded study kits (summaries, concepts, action items, flashcards, quiz questions, and Q&A).
- **No Third-Party Transmission**: StudyLens AI does not send your documents or personal data to any external third-party tracking, analytics, or third-party advertising services.

---

### 2. Data Storage
- **Local Storage**: Analyses and study kits are saved to your local user directory at `~/.anna-study-analyses.json`.
- **User Control**: You have full control over your saved history: you can reopen, rename, search, or permanently delete any analysis at any time from the **History** tab in the app.
- **No Remote Telemetry**: The app does not collect telemetry, analytics cookies, or usage tracking metrics.
