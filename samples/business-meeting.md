ninety seconds. I will split analysis, generation, verification, and saving

failed step. The long-document test will use a cursor rather than sending
the whole document repeatedly.

passes the Unicode, timeout, and history-restore checks by 14 October. If it
does not, we move the launch to 1 November. Elena will run the accessibility
review on 12 October. Jon will coordinate three beta users on 15 October.

Action summary: Ravi owns engineering fixes and the release candidate;
Elena owns interface states and accessibility; Jon owns marketing assets and
beta coordination; Maya owns final product and copy approval. The team will
meet again on 9 October to review progress and unresolved risks.
